Product Overview
================

|Product| is a 10.1” tablet built for the most demanding environments. It is powered by a 12th Gen |Intel| |Core| processor, and its sunlight-readable display stays readable outdoors.

The rugged aluminum alloy chassis is IP65 rated against dust and water, and the tablet works reliably in extreme temperatures. Optionally, it can be made resistant to mold, salt spray and damp heat, and to transportation vibration, vibration and shock, and it can comply with MIL-STD EMC requirements. Its quick-release I/O expansion modules add USB, Ethernet, serial or scanner ports when needed.

:pagebreak:`True`

Ordering Options
================

Most of the Chipsee products can be customized during the ordering process. The product will be shipped with the factory defaults if no extra requirements are specified. The table in the `Hardware Features`_ section lists the default and optional features of the product.

Operating System
----------------

|product| supports Microsoft® Windows 10/11 Pro 64-bit, KylinOS V10 and Linux.

Optional Features
-----------------

By default, |product| has no wireless communication module installed. Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, 5G/4G LTE and GPS/GLONASS/BeiDou modules can be selected during the ordering process, as well as the cameras, the fingerprint reader and the expansion modules.
