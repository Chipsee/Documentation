Product Overview
================

|Product| is a 15.6” laptop built for the most demanding environments. It is powered by a 12th to 14th Gen |Intel| |Core| processor, optionally with an NVIDIA® graphics card, and its sunlight-readable display stays readable outdoors.

The rugged aluminum alloy casing is IP65 rated against dust and water, and the laptop works reliably in extreme temperatures. Optionally, it can be made resistant to mold, salt spray and damp heat, and to transportation vibration, vibration and shock, and it can comply with MIL-STD EMC requirements. A wide range of communication modules can be built in.

:pagebreak:`True`

Ordering Options
================

Most of the Chipsee products can be customized during the ordering process. The product will be shipped with the factory defaults if no extra requirements are specified. The table in the `Hardware Features`_ section lists the default and optional features of the product.

Operating System
----------------

|product| supports Microsoft® Windows 10/11 Pro 64-bit.

Optional Features
-----------------

By default, |product| has no wireless communication module installed. Wi-Fi 6E, Bluetooth 5.2, 5G/4G LTE and GPS/BeiDou modules can be selected during the ordering process, as well as the graphics card, the touch screen, the camera and the fingerprint reader.
